Our Story

Founded with Vision,
Built with Passion

Café Heighné was founded in 2026 by Hanylyn Rivera, a 3rd year BS in Computer Engineering student with a vision of creating a café brand that blends elegance, comfort, and identity into one memorable experience.

More than just a coffee concept, Café Heighné represents creativity, ambition, and personal expression. From its visual branding to its curated menu and merchandise, every detail was thoughtfully designed to reflect warmth, sophistication, and modern café culture.
